Evaporation, Condensation, and Precipitation are the main steps of the water cycle.

Evaporation: The Sun heats water in rivers, lakes, and oceans, causing it to change into water vapor and rise into the air.
Condensation: As the water vapor rises, it cools and changes into tiny water droplets, forming clouds.
Precipitation: When the clouds become heavy with water droplets, the water falls back to Earth as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
